# Yagoya Brand Continuity

Yagoya is a naming and positioning evolution, not a visual rebrand.

## Locked visual identity

The existing visual identity remains the source of truth:

- existing orange thumbs-up / food logo mark
- existing orange patterned splash artwork
- existing orange-led UI palette
- existing visual rhythm, component language and overall look and feel

The current logo and splash files are the same approved source artwork carried forward from the pre-Yagoya build. Only their filenames were changed to match the current product name; their image contents are unchanged.

## Naming rule

Current user-facing product naming and messaging use **Yagoya**. Historical migration/release notes may retain earlier names where context requires it.

## Product positioning

Visual continuity does not dilute the current Yagoya proposition: **We tell you where the good food is.** Recommendation, trust, quality monitoring and predictability are product behavior, not a reason to redesign the established visual identity.

## Change control

Future builds must not replace, redraw, recolor or materially restyle the approved logo, splash artwork, palette or overall UI identity unless a deliberate visual rebrand is explicitly approved.
